# Phase 3 — Better Worksheets (2026-11-09)

## Scope and rationale
Build on the verified existing printer-friendly HTML/PDF-by-print paper flow, without a new
PDF dependency or a parallel publication pipeline. Reuse checked original practice questions,
anonymous earlier authentic assessment **format** evidence, the existing letter/bubble modes,
scope gates, study/score storage, and source separation. Do not reproduce publisher questions
or upload learner work.

## Behavior
- When an **actual prior teacher test** shows a word-bank format (e.g. Religion Chapter 1),
  a checked original practice sample with two or more unique short answers renders the
  original prompts as answer-on-line items with a distinct reordered answer bank.
  Ambiguous answers, repeated answers, prompt-leaked answers, or missing format evidence
  revert to the original multiple-choice or written-response form. Do not claim the next test
  will use a word bank.
- A compact Math paper (up to four questions, excluding existing bubble-layout papers) whose verified test topic is place value/expanded form may include an **empty**
  hundreds/tens/ones scratch chart for 1–3 digit problems. Supported subtraction within 20
  may include a number line; larger or unrelated problems get no invented model.
- Every question retains its independently checked answer in the **separate adult key**;
  explanatory hints stay there rather than appearing on the child's paper.
- Keep existing U.S. Letter print path, in-app phone preview, explicit print action,
  and the teacher-format provenance warning. The app does not download or save PDFs
  silently: users may save as PDF from the standard print dialog.

## Acceptance and limits
Run Node/regression, browser/phone, print-media/PDF, source/privacy and full protected QA.
The browser-generated PDF is evidence of *print-to-PDF*, not evidence of a separate PDF
export implementation. The previous real test is a format reference, not a prediction.
Missing teacher facts and source-pending guides must remain unfilled. Physical printer
and phone acceptance must not be claimed absent hands-on testing.
