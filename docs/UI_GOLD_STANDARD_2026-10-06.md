## Visual system

The app should feel like one authored native-style school companion:

- ABVM navy is the structural anchor.
- Bright sky blue, gold, pink, green and purple are purposeful accents.
- White and very light tinted surfaces carry most content.
- Cards use consistent radii, borders, spacing and restrained depth.
- Headers are short, bold and immediately scannable.
- Icons and subject accents use one consistent visual vocabulary.
- The approved real ABVM school photography remains part of Today/Home and Family.
- Bottom navigation remains compact, stable and obvious.
- Study Games uses compact color-coded rows; the question player focuses attention on progress, the question, any teaching visual, answers, feedback and one dominant next action.
- Calendar can remain the quietest surface, but shares the same tokens and component quality.
- Color must never be the only carrier of meaning.

## Weekly summary

The Week surface includes a compact `This week at a glance` block with two lists:

1. `Lunches this week`: every Monday-Friday school day, including explicit no-school/no-lunch/not-yet-verified states.
2. `Tests this week`: every governed test or assessment in the displayed school week, grouped by date, with a clear empty state when none exist.

Both lists are projections of the existing governed pack. They must not become a second manually maintained source of truth.

## Acceptance

Visual review uses real rendered phone and tablet evidence and includes 200% text, safe areas, landscape and reduced motion. Functional/source/accessibility gates remain mandatory. No visual change may weaken school-data provenance, learning/scoring semantics, privacy, offline behavior or existing retry/recovery contracts.
